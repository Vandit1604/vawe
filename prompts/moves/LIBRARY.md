# prompts/moves/LIBRARY.md: the move library

**A move earns its place with three things: a real reference, the owner's approval of its clip, and a
clear use.** A move without all three does not belong here. Removing a move is normal: a clip the
owner does not approve, or a use another move covers better, goes out.

Status: **built** has a snippet, a demo and a clip in this folder (see [README.md](README.md)).
**to build** is approved as a name and a use only.
