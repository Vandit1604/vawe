# License FAQ

Vawe uses the **[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE)**: a permissive, OSI-approved open source
license. The short version:

> **Free to use, modify, distribute, and sell, for any purpose, including production and
> commercial use. No paid tier. No team-size limit.**

## What may I do with Vawe?

- **Use it**, for any purpose, including inside a commercial product.
- **Modify it**, and keep your changes private or share them.
- **Distribute it**, as-is or modified, including as part of a paid product.
- **Sublicense it**, as part of a larger work.

## What does the patent grant do?

Every contributor grants you a license to any patent claims they hold that their contribution
would infringe. If you sue anyone claiming Vawe infringes a patent, your patent license under
this project ends. This protects users from a contributor asserting a patent later against the
same code they contributed.

## What must I do in return?

- **Keep the copyright and license notice**: include a copy of the `LICENSE` file in any
  distribution, in source or compiled form.
- **State your changes**: if you modify a file, note that you changed it.
- **Carry the `NOTICE` file forward**: if this repo's `NOTICE` file has content, include it (or
  its attribution text) in your distribution. See `NOTICE` and `CREDITS.md` for what that covers.

## Is there a warranty?

No. Vawe is provided "AS IS", without warranty of any kind. See the [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the
full disclaimer of warranty and limitation of liability.

## Can I fork it and contribute?

Yes. Forking, modifying, and opening pull requests are all fine. Contributions are welcome, and
by contributing you license your contribution under the same Apache License 2.0.

## What about the fonts and other bundled assets?

No fonts are bundled with Vawe. `make fonts` downloads the free, openly-licensed faces (OFL/Apache)
from their own sources at build time. Paid faces (e.g. Söhne) are never distributed, you supply your
own copy locally. See [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) for the full list of third-party assets and their
licenses; each stays under its own license regardless of the Apache License covering Vawe's own code.
