principle: The hard cut is the default seam. A transition earns its place by naming what it does.
limit: none
range: hard cuts are 50 to 95 percent of seams, by pace: the fast end for a kinetic film, the low end for a calm one; one primary family for most seams plus 2 to 3 earned accents
break-when: never
instead: cut on the beat or two frames early. Use a dissolve for passing time, a fade for an act break, a whip for momentum, a zoom to enter a surface, a match cut for a hidden connection, a morph for a logo or one hero moment.
check: judge
judge: What does each visible transition mean? Could it be a hard cut?
prevents: doc TRANSITIONS and TASTE-RULES: a showy transition on every seam is the amateur tell; blur was not wrong as a primary, it was wrong as the only thing. The 95 percent figure is the fast end of the range, not a measured fact.
