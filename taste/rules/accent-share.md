---
id: accent-share
step: look
principle: One accent hue per film, scarce and visible. It colours the thing the beat is about. It never floods the frame.
limit: no frame where the accent covers the whole frame
range: accent 10 percent of a frame (up to about 25 percent for atmosphere); neutrals fill the rest; semantic colours stay distinct from the accent
break-when: the brand's own palette is the flood and the brief says so (off-colour waiver with a reason)
instead: put the accent on one thing: a caret, an eyebrow, one numeral, the CTA. On an accent-coloured ground, do not use the accent for emphasis (blue on blue vanishes).
check: judge, accent-flood
judge: Is there a frame where the accent covers the whole frame? Does each accent colour decorate something?
