principle: Land the incoming subject where the eye was on the outgoing frame. Do not make the eye cross the frame at a seam. Plan the eye path per beat: where the eye starts, what pulls it (name the device), where it lands, and every device (colour flash, reveal, push, cursor, contrast, focus pull) names its target.
limit: the incoming subject lands within 0.30 of the frame diagonal of the outgoing focal point
range: one landing per moment; motion pulls first, then colour or size settles it
break-when: a cut that serves the story outranks the eye: declare it in the authoring block with a reason; a held beat can keep the eye where the cut put it
instead: in order of cost: place the next headline where the last focal point was; aim the outgoing beat at the corner the next beat opens in; give the next beat more time; keep the jump and say why. Within a beat, move the eye with light (a glow that arrives cold and wide, then tightens and goes hot), with focus (rack from one plane to the other) or with a beam used as a mask; a glow parked on the hero for the whole beat stops being seen within a second.
