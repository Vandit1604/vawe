id: cut-on-beat
step: transitions
principle: Cut on the beat, or two frames early, on an action; never in a dead hold. Cut where the audience would blink: on the completed idea.
limit: none
range: on the beat or 2 frames early; match-on-action overlap of 2 to 5 frames
break-when: there is no music file or beat
instead: cut on the action frame (a word lands, a count finishes). Read a user-given track with `vawe sound <track> --cuts <page>`: it gives the beat grid and hits with frame numbers and the second to move each off cut to. If the logo lands at 24:15, pick the tempo that puts a beat there; do not nudge the logo.
check: cut-off-beat
judge: Does each cut land on the beat or an action?
prevents: doc MOTION-CRAFT and SOUND: on-beat cuts read directed, off-beat ones read sloppy.
status: active
scored: no
numbers: {"early_frames":2,"match_overlap_min_frames":2,"match_overlap_max_frames":5}
