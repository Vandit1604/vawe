principle: One thread carries through the film: an object, a type line, a colour or a rhythm. Carry it in two registers (one literal, one subtle) so no single one has to be obvious.
limit: none
range: 1 to 2 threads, named in the brief: an object, a type line, a colour or a rhythm
break-when: a manifesto or an anthology is held by the voice and the pulse; say so in the brief
instead: name the thread before the first still. Object: the caret writes, then expands into the panel. Type line: the headline's last word is the next shot's first. Colour: one accent passes from a word to a ground to a button. Rhythm: every cut lands on one pulse. Unfinished sentence: no clause finishes at a cut.
check: judge
