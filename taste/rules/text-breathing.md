principle: Words need rests. A film with readable text in every frame gives the eye no place to look at the picture.
limit: readable text on screen at most 84 percent of the film
range: reference films show text 3 to 84 percent of the time (p10 to p90), median 61
break-when: a kinetic type film where the words are the picture; name it in the brief
instead: leave one or two beats with no words: let the product, the object or the ground carry them. Cut text from the beat that says the same thing twice.
check: text-breathing
judge: Mark the tiles with no readable text. Is there at least one stretch of them?

## Example

A 20 s film shows text for 16 s at most; the 4 s left show the product alone.

Draft check: The draft check counts the samples of the whole film (one every 0.5 s or more) that hold a line with a height of 2 percent of the frame or more. Text inside aria-hidden or data-chrome is left out only when it is texture by measure (cap height under 2 percent, the same words 3 or more times, or a data-texture reason); any other hidden text counts as copy. A window draft and a film under 10 s do not run it.
