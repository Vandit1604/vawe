step: look
principle: A claim that is a quantity, a proportion, a change over time, a flow or a real thing wants its own graphic, not a number set in type.
limit: none
range: none
break-when: a line that is none of the five claim shapes is fine as type
instead: quantity: a bar whose length is the figure (a count-up beside it, never instead). Proportion: a ring, an arc, a filled grid. Change: a line or a before and after side by side. Flow: nodes and connectors that draw on. Real thing: a capture, a photo, a clipped screenshot.
check: judge
judge: Which claim in the film is a quantity, proportion, change, flow or real thing set only in type?
prevents: doc SHOW-DONT-TELL.md: a number in type is a word; a bar is a size the eye compares without reading. The missing 17 percent shows in a ring and hides in a numeral.

## Example

"83 percent" is a ring with an arc, and the 17 percent gap is visible.
