limit: a sheen band, lens streak, light beam, accent bar or rule line at most once per film

break-when: the brief asks for it, or the content calls for it (a real sequence the viewer follows, a real product screen): declare it in the page (authoring.allow with a _why)
instead: gradient text: one flat colour from the kit. Corner labels and borders: nothing decorates the edge. Glow on UI text: contrast from weight and size. Bursts, rings, split and shake: the subject itself moving. Centred title on a gradient: the title on a real surface, a capture or white space. Template chrome: drop the eyebrow and let the title carry the beat; number only a true sequence; mark a card with space or a surface step, not a stripe; vary the cards in size and weight. A stock device: let the subject carry the beat; a different look, shader or 3D toy every beat is effect soup.

## Example

Wrong: a corner timecode label; "FEATURES" in tracked caps above the title, then three equal rounded cards. Right: no label; the title, then one real capture and one sheen across the logo.

Draft check, template-chrome: a card has a surface, a radius of 6 px or more, 3 percent of the frame or more, and two or more children.
