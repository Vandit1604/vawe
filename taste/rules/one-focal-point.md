---
id: one-focal-point
step: look
principle: One focal point per frame and one accent colour per frame. The brightest and largest thing in the frame is the subject. The eye ranks brighter, then larger, then in focus, then moving.
limit: none
range: one focal point off-centre, one accent colour
break-when: a deliberate two-part frame where the second thing arrives after the first lands
instead: let the second thing arrive after the first lands. Brighter means contrast against the ground, not luminance: white type on a white field is not the focal point.
check: judge
judge: Which one thing is the focal point, and is it the brightest and largest?
prevents: feedback 2026-09-11: "every device points the eye". doc banned-defaults: two things fighting for attention; everything centred at equal weight.
status: active
scored: yes
numbers: {}
print-frames: give each frame one focal point and one accent colour, not two things fighting
craft: layout
---

## Example

Right: a large white headline off-centre, one cobalt word. Wrong: three equal cards, all centred.
