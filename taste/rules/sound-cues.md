step: sound
principle: Sync points are scarce. Tie each cue to one visible event, spend one or two hard syncs on the moments you want believed, and keep at most one soft swell that ends on a cut. A whoosh on every transition makes every transition equally important, so none is.
limit: at most 1 swell per film; 1 to 2 hard sync points per short film
range: impact on a hard stop, never on a soft EASE.land settle; UI tick on an operated interface; whoosh only on a layer that really crosses the frame; riser for the one thing about to come; for a 10 to 40 s film with a user-given track, lock the track first and cut on its grid
break-when: a sound bridge or a user-given track carries the film (see sound-bridge)
instead: name the visible event behind each cue and cut a cue with none. Time each cue to the page's own beat number (the literal the CSS or seek uses), so a retimed beat moves both. A swell stops dead on the cut it introduces (no tail); a riser ends on the beat and leaves the frame after it quiet. Wall-to-wall hits turn into noise.
check: judge
judge: Name the visible event behind each cue. Is there a tick on every word, a whoosh on every cut, or more than one swell?
prevents: feedback "use subtle sounds"; doc SOUND (Chion's synchresis): a sound and an image that meet fuse into one event; a loud repeated pluck reads as a machine gun; two cues in one frame read as a mistake.

print-check: tie each cue to one visible event and cue the first and the last key word, not a tick on every word
