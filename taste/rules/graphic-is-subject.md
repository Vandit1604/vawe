---
id: graphic-is-subject
step: look
principle: If a graphic is the point of the beat, it owns the frame: hero scale, type demoted to a caption. A graphic below the floor reads as a mark.
limit: a graphic that carries a beat covers at least 8 percent of the canvas (about a 400 by 400 box)
range: at least one carrying graphic in the film, on screen for at least a third of the runtime
break-when: a film whose whole idea is type on a field, decided on purpose
instead: bring the picture forward and make the type a caption. A picture behind the content is scenery.
check: judge
judge: Is one graphic large enough to be the subject of its beat, and on screen for a third of the film?
prevents: doc SHOW-DONT-TELL.md: a 60 px logo beside a headline is punctuation. Median film gave about 5 percent of its layers to picture.
status: active
scored: no
