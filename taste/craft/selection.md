# SELECTION: intent, then the right choice, and why

[motion-craft.md](motion-craft.md) is effect-first ("a whip means momentum"). This page is intent-first: you know the
feeling a beat wants, and it says which cut, face, ease and colour is correct, with a reason.
[failure-modes.md](failure-modes.md) says what is bad. This says what to pick.

Every choice signals two things: how much the viewer should notice it, and what register it evokes. A cut
hides the seam or announces it (Murch). A face is trusted, fresh or technical. An ease is an arrival or a
departure. Pick by the signal you want to send.

## Transitions

Ask three questions. Should the viewer notice the edit? No: hard cut (the default, [hard-cut-default](../rules/hard-cut-default.md)) or a soft
dissolve. Yes, the edit is part of the show: whip, wipe, iris. What does the seam mean? Does the ground
jump between dark and light? If so, cover it with an effect that peaks at the cut.

| the beat wants | reach for | why |
|---|---|---|
| invisible continuity | hard cut | the most basic edit; preserves narrative flow |
| time passing, reflection | dissolve | a dissolve implies time passing (Murch) |
| an act break, finality | fade to dark | marks a significant story break |
| energy, momentum | whip pan | motion blur masks the cut |
| playful, "notice me" | wipe | dynamic; draws attention to the cut |
| a hidden connection | match the framing across a hard cut | a match cut reveals a connection |
| product focus | push-in or punch | scale draws the eye to one subject |
| a dramatic pivot | a heavy transition | a heavy transition earns a heavy story turn |

One cut family per film: mixing whip and iris is five fonts on a slide ([hard-cut-default](../rules/hard-cut-default.md)).
Adjacent moving transitions change axis or direction ([seam-variety](../rules/seam-variety.md)). Full theory and
per-seam procedure: [transitions.md](transitions.md).

## Faces

Register first: authority, tradition or luxury is serif. Modern and clean is sans (humanist sans tests
highest for trust, geometric sans reads fresh and cold). Technical, retro or a terminal is mono. Strong
editorial headline is slab or heavy sans. Human and intimate is script, sparingly. Pick two or three
roles that contrast without conflicting: a serif head over a sans body, never a formal serif with a
quirky script. Details: [typography.md](typography.md).

## Easing

EASE.land for arrivals, EASE.launch for departures, never linear on a visible move, and EASE.pop only for a press
or a pop the page names. A novice reads bounce as emphasis. It reads as cheap ([overshoot](../rules/overshoot.md),
[named-eases](../rules/named-eases.md)).

## Reference profiles: name the target, get the whole look

An adjective is vague. A brand is a spec. Pick the one reference the film should feel like, and every
family follows coherently. This is the taste anchor when there is no brand site. When there is one, take
its palette and face and apply the profile's motion policy.

| profile | for | face | pace and ease | cuts | effects | accent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| linear | dev tools, dark, restrained | mono and tight sans | fast, snappy (EASE.land, short) | hard cuts | almost none; one at the hero | one cool accent |
| apple | product launch, calm | clean sans, one hero per beat | slow, smooth, 0.5 to 0.9 s | dissolves, never whips | one glamour moment; held frames | one, often none |
| stripe | developer brand, warm-serious | humanist sans | smooth, confident | soft blur | a soft gradient field behind | gradient accents |
| nike | sports ad, kinetic | heavy sans or slab; kinetic type is the star | punchy (EASE.land on a fast band), motion blur on | whip, punch | on reveals | one loud accent on black or white |
| a24 | trailer, dramatic | serif or stark condensed sans | slow, long holds | fade, letterbox | grain throughout, one big turn | one muted accent |
| bloomberg | data and finance, dense | mono and sans | fast, mechanical | collapse, punch | counters and charts are the content | amber or green |
| duolingo | consumer app, playful | rounded sans | bouncy: the one profile where overshoot is right | wipe, iris | confetti on wins | several bright accents |
| vercel | keynote, black, sharp | geometric sans and mono | snappy but spare | hard cuts only | one glitch or split at the single reveal | white plus one |

Bounce is wrong on every profile except duolingo. That exception is what the other seven prove.

Choose by decision, not by menu. An agent read the anti-default doctrine in full, picked a theme off a
menu of 34 and wrote the reasoning afterwards. The test: name the profile you picked, then describe the
content that would make a different profile correct. If you cannot, you recognised one, you did not
choose between eight. Reject your first instinct: it is usually your training-data default for that
register. For whole directions, not single choices, use the pitch round (`engine-doctrine/CRAFT/PITCH.md`).

## Contradictions to flag

- Bounce on linear, apple, vercel or a24: cheap on a serious brand.
- A whip or wipe on apple or a24: it announces an edit a calm or tense film wants hidden.
- A script or serif on linear or bloomberg: wrong register (technical wants mono).
- More than one cut family in a film.
- A texture or effect on every beat. Effects are earned two or three times: the hero reveal, an act
  break, the call to action. They are never wallpaper.
- A loud transition effect on apple or linear: restraint is the brand.
