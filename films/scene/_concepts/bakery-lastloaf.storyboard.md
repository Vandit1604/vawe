# The bakery that sells out before lunch

> A FIXTURE, not a film in the queue. It is the second brief `make invent-look` is tested against:
> a look that suits this and also suits a GitHub year in review is a look that suits anything.

SUBJECT: Aylmer Street Bakery, a two-person neighbourhood bakery whose sourdough is gone by 11am
DATA: the shop's own till export, Jan to Jun 2026, pasted below
PAYOFF: they bake 40 loaves a day and could sell 300, and they will not bake one more
AUDIENCE: locals on Instagram, plus anyone who has queued outside a bakery in the rain
FEELING: worn, domestic, hand-lettered. Nothing shiny.

## The data

| fact | value |
|---|---|
| loaves a day | 40 |
| sold out by | 11:04am, on average |
| starter age | 9 years |
| queue, Saturday | 22 people at 7am |
| flour, one mill | Shipton Mill, 63 miles away |
| **payoff** | **they turn away 260 customers a day, on purpose** |

## Beats

**1 · the oven door opens.** Steam. A single loaf, huge in frame.
**2 · the count.** 40 loaves roll up. The shelf fills.
**3 · the queue.** 22 people at 7am, shown as a line, not a number.
**4 · 11:04am.** The shelf is empty. The clock is the picture.
**5 · the payoff.** 260 customers turned away, on purpose, because the starter sets the pace.
