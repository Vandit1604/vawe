# Nine effects, and what each one costs by hand

DATA: the presets themselves, and the standard After Effects technique for each
PAYOFF: every one of them is a single word of JSON here
AUDIENCE: motion designers and developers evaluating the engine
FEELING: a workshop reference sheet. A parts catalogue, not a showreel.

## 1 - the hook  (0.0s - 3.9s)

- type: hook
- onscreen: "After Effects charges by the layer." | "Nine effects, costed by hand."
- why: name the price model the whole film argues with, before showing a single effect.
- layout: the line fills the middle band of the frame, left aligned against the grid; the lower third stays empty on purpose.
- style: the still workshop grid, light. The line at weight 800.
- mechanism: `split: word` + `preset: up`.
- becomes: an empty grid becomes a claim with a number attached to it.
- rest: none. The hook holds.
