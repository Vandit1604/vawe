# SFX CATALOG: the 13 synthesized cues, and when to reach for each

## AGENT SUMMARY

- Reach for a real cue instead of shipping mute. All 13 cues below are synthesized on the fly by
  `core/audio/kit.mjs`: no files, no license, nothing that 404s on a fresh clone.
- Pick by family and placement, not by name alone: an accent is not a transition, and a riser must be
  hand-timed to end on its beat, never auto-mapped to a cut.
- Enforced by `make audio-check` (code `silence-without-a-reason`): a film that ships `audio.silent:true`
  must say why. Regenerate this doc with `node harness/author/sfx-catalog.mjs`; do not hand-edit it.

| Cue | Family | Energy | Purpose | Placement | Pitfall |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pluck` | accent | low | punctuate something small landing: a counter digit, a minor element arriving | hand-placed cue, or auto via CUT_CUE for jitter/clock/blinds/barn cuts | loud and repeated reads as a machine gun (the file's own words) |
| `chime` | accent | low | a light confirmation tone for a small positive event | hand-placed cue | none noted |
| `sparkle` | accent | low | a bright four-note flourish for a decorative reveal | hand-placed cue, or auto via SEAM_CUE for dispersion/shatterGlitch/pixelDissolve seams | none noted |
| `droplet` | accent | low | a two-tone accent for a single discrete arrival | hand-placed cue, or auto via CUT_CUE for drop/zoom/blur cuts, SEAM_CUE for lens/rippleWave seams | none noted |
| `bloom` | confirm | low | a soft two-tone open for something gently arriving or expanding into view | hand-placed cue, or auto via CUT_CUE for softiris/iris/rise/riseBlur/fade/matchCut cuts, SEAM_CUE for fade/dissolve/sdfIris/portal/irisRound/lumaWipe seams | none noted |
| `success` | confirm | medium | a three-note ascending confirmation for a completed, positive event | hand-placed cue | none noted |
| `ready` | confirm | low | a two-tone signal that something is now available or armed | hand-placed cue | none noted |
| `whoosh` | transition | medium | an object or camera passing by: the frame carries something across itself | auto on a moving cut via CUT_CUE (whip/skewWhip/softwipe/wipe/slide/push/flip/spin/cube/roll), auto on a moving seam via SEAM_CUE (slide/uncover/wipe/crossWarp/whipPan/barnDoor/clockWipe/zoomBlur/swirlWarp/blindsWipe/spinZoom), or a bridge at a cut/seam | a one-directional sweep with no fall after the peak sounds like a jet that never arrives; this voicing sweeps up then down so the pass has an inflection |
| `riser` | riser | high | a build INTO a moment, tension climbing toward a beat | hand-placed, must be timed to END on the beat it feeds, never auto-mapped | without the counter-moving sub layer the build thins out exactly where it should get heavier; the engine also cannot cut the level at the peak the way a real riser recipe wants, so leave the frame after it quiet |
| `drop` | impact | high | a sub-bass downlifter that lands ON a cut rather than before it | hand-placed cue, or auto via CUT_CUE for the drop cut style, SEAM_CUE for cinematicZoom | landing above 60Hz reads as a bass note rather than a room-sized drop; this voicing is the half-second cinematic sub-down, not the fast 808 knock |
| `impact` | impact | high | a hit: something collides, lands, or slams into place | hand-placed cue, or auto via CUT_CUE for punch/squeeze/collapse/letterbox cuts, SEAM_CUE for push/flashWhite/burnThrough seams | building only the body reads as a thump behind a curtain; only the transient reads as a click. This voicing layers both plus a 1-3kHz mid so the three read as one event |
| `swell` | weight | high | the reverse-cymbal move: a sound accelerating into a cut and stopping dead on it | hand-placed cue, timed to end exactly on the cut it introduces | a fade at the end reads as a fade-out rather than an arrival; this voicing carries no tail so the collapse lands within about 90ms of the cut |
| `braam` | weight | high | low sustained weight for a serious, dramatic moment (a scaled-down version of the trailer horn) | hand-placed cue | this engine has no distortion, stereo field or resonator, so treat it as low sustained weight, not the full trailer braam |

## Provenance

Every cue's synthesis spec lives in `core/audio/kit.mjs` (`CUES`), voicings ported from Cuelume
(MIT, Daniel Belyi, https://github.com/Danilaa1/cuelume). This document is generated by
`harness/author/sfx-catalog.mjs` from that same registry. Do not hand-edit it: edit the `META` map in
the script, then run `node harness/author/sfx-catalog.mjs` to regenerate.
