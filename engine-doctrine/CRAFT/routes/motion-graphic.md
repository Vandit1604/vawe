# Route: motion-graphic

- **Input:** one word, mark, stat, or short phrase; no narration, no talking product tour.
- **Output:** a short, motion-first unit, typically under 10s: a logo sting, a stat hit, a title card
  that moves, a lower-third. Showreel pace (1.5-4s per idea).
- **Trigger:** "a N second sting", "logo reveal", "stat hit", "title card that moves", any request
  that is explicitly short and says the motion itself is the message, with no voice-over. A static
  (non-animated) title card is not this route; a longer narrated piece is not this route either.

## Intake (ask before authoring)

- **The one move**: what single motion carries the whole unit (a word blasting in, a logo lockup, a
  count-up)? A motion-graphic with two competing moves is really two beats fighting for one slot.
- **Loop or one-shot?** Decides whether the end state resets or holds.
- **Brand marks**: logo, colour, and the exact word or number, since there is no room for anything
  else to establish them.
- **Destination**: aspect ratio, since a sting is often reused across several feeds.

## Blueprint family + docs

- Read `engine-doctrine/CRAFT/KEYED-MOTION.md` and "AUTHOR THE MOTION. DO NOT NAME IT." in `AGENTS.md`: a unit
  this short is judged entirely on its hand-keyed motion track, never a named preset.
- `engine-doctrine/CRAFT/SPECIMEN.md` if the point is to prove one mechanism works rather than to ship a film
  (`make demo`).
- Blueprint family: `kineticHook` or `statReveal` alone, never chained with unrelated beats.
