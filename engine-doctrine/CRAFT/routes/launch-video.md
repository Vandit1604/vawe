# Route: launch-video

- **Input:** a URL to a real product or site, or a brief that names one. `make sections` (whole site,
  not just the homepage) and `make palette` capture its assets, colours and fonts before you write a
  frame.
- **Output:** a promo / launch film that markets the product, built on its own captured UI and copy.
  Sweet spot 20-60s.
- **Trigger:** the request names a real product, company, or site to market or launch, or says
  "promo", "launch video", "market our X". If the ask is instead to reflect a real FILM or another
  site's exact look with no product to sell, that is `recreation.md`, not this route.

## Intake (ask before authoring)

- **Which URL, and every page?** One page never shows the real product; crawl routes, view modes and
  empty states before storyboarding (`AGENTS.md` "Check EVERY page").
- **STYLE ANCHOR**: the site itself supplies palette/type/motion. Confirm which pages to feature if the
  site is large.
- **Payoff**: the one thing to remember, landing last. **Audience** and **destination** (aspect ratio),
  if not already given.
- **SPECTACLE + NOT**: name the one exaggerated beat, and what this film explicitly will not do.

## Blueprint family + docs

- Load skill `vawe-type` (`reference/launch.md`) for the launch playbook: spine, motion to reach for,
  the rules that matter most.
- Storyboard from `RECREATION.md` step 2 (capture-first, one beat per real section) and
  `SHOW-DONT-TELL.md` (the product must be shown working, not only claimed).
- Launch-specific standing rules live in `AGENTS.md` under "Launch-video rules": logo prominence,
  paired directional enter/exit, blur-out for dense UI, a changing word in a fixed box.
- Motion: a feature-grid and a statement recipe/device for feature and proof beats; reach for `make arsenal Q="…"`
  before hand-authoring motion.
