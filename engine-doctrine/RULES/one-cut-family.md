# One transition family per film; earn 2-3 accents by meaning

A film that mixes wipes, dissolves, whip pans and hard cuts with no reason reads as indecisive. Pick
one family (a hard cut is a valid family) and use it for most seams. Reserve 2-3 accent transitions
for seams that carry meaning, such as a tone flip or a time jump, and be able to say which meaning
each one serves. Adjacent transitions still change axis or direction (AGENTS.md).
