# Pick a named speed band; the slowest beat is at least 3x the fastest

Duration is a voice, not a constant. A film that uses one band reads as monotone narration. The
slowest beat runs at least 3x the fastest, and no two layers moving independently in one beat share
one ease curve.

| band | duration |
|---|---|
| energy | 0.15 - 0.3 s |
| professional | 0.3 - 0.5 s |
| gravity | 0.5 - 0.8 s |
| cinematic | 0.8 - 2.0 s |

Right: a 0.25 s hook entrance, then a 1.0 s reveal. Wrong: every entrance at 0.45 s.
