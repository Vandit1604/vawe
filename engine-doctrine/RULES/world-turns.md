# The backdrop changes tone per beat, or the film is a slide with effects on it

The backdrop is always a decision, never a default. Change it at the cuts the film already has: a
pictorial beat on a dead backdrop is still a slide. 82% of the library scenes measured painted one
backdrop for the whole runtime.

To check by hand: two backdrops "turn" when they differ in more than their time span (colour, tone or
pattern). One backdrop for the whole film, or every backdrop the same, means the tone never turns.
Carry the change across the join (a colour that follows the content, or a crossfade timed to the
cut), never a cold flash from a light frame to a dark one.
