# Taste card digest (authors)

One line per rule of `TASTE-CARD.md`. The judge scores the full card; read it for the why and the five frames.

1. Subject in frame 0 (by 0.1 s); never an empty ground for 0.5 s.
2. Turn the world every 1-2 beats; nothing static in the last 1 s.
3. One thread carries through (object, type line, colour or rhythm); show real things, not slogans.
4. Every mark rides its parent; no stray dot.
5. Entrances ease out hard; exits shorter and accelerating; no ease-in entrance, no scale(0).
6. Real physics: spring or exponential as 8+ stop keyframes or linear(); not one cubic-bezier.
7. Reveal masks pad 0.3 em past the glyphs; no cut descenders.
8. Speed bands (0.15-0.3, 0.3-0.5, 0.5-0.8 s); slowest beat 3x the fastest; stagger 30-80 ms.
9. Legible: cap height 6%+ of frame, hold max(1.2 s, words/3); add hold, never slow the move.
10. Each seam changes axis or direction and lands near the eye (0.30 diagonal).
11. One focal point, one accent, colour on 1-2 words, each device names its target.
12. vawe films: ground #16151a, white, cobalt sparingly; other brands use the kit; no flood, no #000/#fff.
13. Quiet sound: peak -10 dBFS, about -20 LUFS, ticks plus one soft swell; no impact or riser.
14. No tells: gradient text, corner labels, borders, glow on UI, bursts, RGB split, shake, flares, banding.
15. One entrance per beat, with an origin; not all opacity fades, no bounce.

Attractors (fine only if the direction chose them): glowing orb or disc, dusk sun, lens streak or sheen band, accent bar or rule line, same seam move twice, three directions in one material.
