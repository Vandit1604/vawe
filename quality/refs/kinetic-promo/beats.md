- window: 0.0-3.5s
- technique: chrome 4-point star (three/svgExtrude, metal preset) spins centred on navy+bloom ground, no text

- window: 3.5-10.5s
- technique: single centred hero word per hold ("Clean" / "text" / "animation" / "preset"), blur-in, weight change, turns blue
